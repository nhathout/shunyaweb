How she leads

Translate. Organize. Sustain.

Three habits that recur across the map, and beyond it. Open one to read the evidence.

Making technical and organizational detail clear to the people who have to act on it.

    Translates AI concepts for students in finance, consulting, entrepreneurship, design, data and public policy — then helps them build.

  • Wellington Management

  • Student Government

    Turned organizational updates into accessible, channel-ready messaging for students.
