Six lenses on one case

  1. 01

    Human oversight

    Where does a person stay in the loop, and what do they check?

  2. 02

    Auditability

    Could someone reconstruct what the agent did, and why?

  3. 03

    Data access

    What may the agent see, and who grants that access?

  4. 04

    Output validation

    How is an answer checked before anyone acts on it?

  5. 05

    Deployment readiness

    What must be true before a team relies on it day to day?

  6. 06

    Responsibility allocation

    When the agent gets it wrong, which team owns the outcome?

I treat AI and cybersecurity risks as business risks first, and increasingly as legal ones.
