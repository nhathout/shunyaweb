Research · 2026 – present

Governing Agentic AI in Financial Operations

Independent, faculty-supervised research

A case study of agentic-AI governance in high-stakes financial workflows, grounded in an anonymized trade-operations project at Wellington. It follows six lenses across operational, risk, legal and information-security teams.

Six lenses 01 / 06
01

Human oversight

Where does a person stay in the loop, and what exactly do they check?

02

Auditability

Could someone reconstruct, after the fact, what the agent did and why?

03

Data access

What should the agent be allowed to see, and who grants that access?

04

Output validation

How is an answer checked before anyone acts on it?

05

Deployment readiness

What has to be true before a prototype is trusted with live operations?

06

Responsibility allocation

When the agent gets it wrong, which team owns the outcome?
