what comes next

Sasha’s plan runs in three stages: an MBA with an AI and technology focus, starting Fall 2027; two to three years in consulting; then law school. It leads to a career in AI, cybersecurity, privacy and emerging-technology law.

MBA · consulting · law

The aim

Fluency, not engineering. Sasha wants to understand AI and cybersecurity well enough to help companies use technology responsibly — to manage risk, follow regulation and make better decisions.
