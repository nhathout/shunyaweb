The Next Chapter

An MBA in AI and technology, and the path beyond it

My goal is to help organizations adopt AI responsibly, and to manage the legal, privacy, cybersecurity and business risks that come with it.

The next step is an MBA with an AI and technology focus, beginning in Fall 2027.

See the plan Contact
01The plan

Three stages, in a deliberate order

An MBA first, then consulting, then law school.

  1. Stage 01 · Fall 2027 MBA AI and technology focus: strategy, analytics and how companies decide where AI belongs.
  2. Stage 02 · Two to three years Consulting AI-governance and technology-risk consulting for companies adopting AI.
  3. Stage 03 · Later Law school Legal training for advising where AI, privacy and law meet.

Why the MBA now

The MBA is the core of the plan. It turns my business training and co-op work into the toolkit I need to advise companies on AI.

Why law comes later

Consulting will show me how companies adopt AI and manage technology risk. I want to bring those real cases to legal study.
