Exhibit B · Law school

Why law, why later

Why law

Sasha wants to help companies use AI responsibly and manage cyber and privacy risk. Sooner or later those questions become legal ones — so law is where her path is headed.

Why later

As Sasha sees it, going straight from the MBA to law school would weaken the MBA. Two to three years in consulting will give her real examples of how companies use AI and handle technology risk — a stronger law application, and a stronger legal career.

The law she wants to practice

  • AI regulation
  • Data privacy
  • Algorithmic discrimination
  • Intellectual property
  • Responsible AI
  • Technology contracts
  • Cybersecurity & data-protection law
