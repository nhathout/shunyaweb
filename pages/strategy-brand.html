Editor’s note

Strategy and brand work taught Sasha three disciplines: study the audience, choose a clear position and say it plainly. They are the disciplines AI-governance consulting runs on — explaining a complex technology to the people who have to approve it, use it and answer for it.

What carries into consulting

  1. 01

    Audience insight

    Know who is listening. At TJX, that meant studying audience behavior on social media; at Wellington, translating technical issues for cross-functional teams.

  2. 02

    Positioning

    Choose the one idea that matters. At UMG Technologies, market and customer research became go-to-market recommendations and positioning decisions.

  3. 03

    Clear messaging

    Say it so a non-specialist can repeat it. Today, at NextWork, she translates AI concepts for students in finance, consulting, design and public policy.
